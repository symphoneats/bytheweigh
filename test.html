The hormone specialists.

Endocrinologists are physicians who specialize specifically in the body's hormone systems, the same systems that regulate metabolism, appetite, and blood sugar. That expertise starts with diagnosis: your endocrinologist can assess whether an underlying condition, such as a thyroid disorder, hormonal imbalance, or pituitary issue, is contributing to your weight. GLP-1 medications work by mimicking a hormone the body already produces, precisely the territory endocrinology has specialized in for decades, and endocrinologists were among the first physicians to understand how they work, and among the first to prescribe them.
A GLP-1 medication is one option your endocrinologist may consider, and only when it's medically appropriate.

True integration, not just a referral.

Most patients are left to coordinate their own care, going between a prescriber and a dietitian who rarely speak to each other directly. By The Weigh closes that gap.
Weight is a medical and a metabolic question. That's why By The Weigh pairs a board-certified endocrinologist with a Registered Dietitian in one coordinated plan.
Your physician oversees your labs, hormones, and medication. Your dietitian provides ongoing, month-to-month medical nutrition therapy, continually evaluating your nutrition status and adjusting your plan, not just coaching you through it. Nutrition strategy is built around your medical plan, not bolted on after the fact. That care does two things at once: it makes the medical plan work in real life, and it protects your nutritional adequacy, so losing weight never comes at the cost of the nutrients your body still needs.
