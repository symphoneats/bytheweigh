The hormone specialists

Weight has as much to do with hormones as it does with willpower.

Endocrinologists specialize specifically in the body's hormone systems, the same systems that regulate metabolism, appetite, and blood sugar. Your endocrinologist can assess whether a thyroid disorder, hormonal imbalance, or pituitary issue is contributing to your weight — and GLP-1 medications, which mimic a hormone the body already produces, sit squarely in the territory endocrinology has specialized in for decades.

A GLP-1 medication is one option your endocrinologist may consider — and only when it's medically appropriate.
The nutrition specialists

Losing weight and losing your health are not the same thing.

Registered Dietitians are the clinicians trained specifically in medical nutrition therapy, assessing protein needs, micronutrient status, and how your metabolism shifts as intake drops, not just building meal plans. Appetite falls faster than the body's need for protein, vitamins, and minerals, and left unaddressed, that gap can show up in ways that go beyond the scale, things like hair loss, excessive muscle loss, low energy, and long-term bone loss such as osteopenia or osteoporosis, to name a few. Your dietitian works closely with you throughout the program to keep that gap from becoming severe.

The scale only tells you one piece of the puzzle. Your dietitian makes sure you're not losing the other pieces that keep you healthy.
